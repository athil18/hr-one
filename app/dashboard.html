HROne Enterprise Policy Matrix:
  • R1 Attendance Date: Calendar date in IST (UTC+05:30). Overnight shifts ending next morning belong to previous day's shift.
  • R2 Grace Period: 10-minute grace window. If punched strictly after grace, full elapsed time from shift start is charged.
  • R3 Overtime: Minimum 30 minutes beyond scheduled shift end; otherwise 0.
  • R4 Duration: Rounding using Decimal arithmetic with ROUND_HALF_UP to 2 decimals.
  • R5 Half-Day: Work hours under 4.50 hours flag record as half-day (0.5 present day).
